# Pane Support

Need help? Email [ccloudcreativez3@gmail.com](mailto:ccloudcreativez3@gmail.com). Replies usually come within two working days.

## Pane isn't pausing my apps

1. Make sure Screen Time is on: **Settings › Screen Time**.
2. Open Pane. If it says it can't block apps, tap **Allow Access**. You can also turn Pane on in **Settings › Screen Time › Apps with Screen Time Access**.
3. iOS updates can turn this permission off. Opening Pane after an update lets it check, and restarting your iPhone also helps.

## How do I end a session early?

- **Normal:** tap **End Session** on Home.
- **Timeout:** tap **End Early**, then wait for the 60-second pause to finish.
- **Deep Focus (Pro):** can't be ended early, by design. If you made a Pane Key, scan it with **End with Pane Key**.

## Notifications from paused apps

While an app is paused, its notifications may not arrive until the pause ends.

## Restoring Pane Pro

Go to **Settings › Restore Purchase**, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If Family Sharing is on, family members get Pro too.

## Turning off reminders

Pane's reminders and its Sunday summary are local notifications. Turn them off in **Settings › Notifications › Pane**.

## Deleting your data

Everything Pane knows is stored on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting Pane deletes it all. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

## Privacy

Pane collects no data. Read the full [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
