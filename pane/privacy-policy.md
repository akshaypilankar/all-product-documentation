# Pane Privacy Policy

*Effective 11 October 2026*

Pane is made by Akshay Pilankar. In short: **Pane doesn't collect any data.** Everything you set up in Pane stays on your device.

## What Pane stores, and where

Your block lists, schedules, Mindful Apps settings, session history, milestones and preferences are saved on your device. Only Pane and its own widgets and extensions can read them. They are never sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting Pane deletes them.

## Screen Time

Pane uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to pause the apps you choose.

- **Pane can't see which apps you pick.** Apple gives Pane only coded tokens for the apps, categories and websites you choose.
- **Usage data stays with Apple.** The Screen Time usage Pane shows (in Report and Suggestions) is drawn by Apple's report system in a separate, sealed-off part of the app. That data can't leave it: Pane can't read it, save it or send it anywhere.

## No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

Pane has:
- no sign-in
- no analytics or crash-reporting tools
- no advertising
- no third-party code
- no internet connections of its own

Pane doesn't track you across apps or websites.

## Purchases

Pane Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells Pane only that Pro is unlocked. I never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

## Permissions Pane may ask for

- **Screen Time:** to pause the apps you choose.
- **Notifications** (optional): reminders and alerts created on your device.
- **Camera** (optional): only to scan your own Pane Key QR code. Images aren't saved or sent.
- **Photos, add only** (optional): to save a wallpaper or your Pane Key image when you ask.

## Email

"Email to Yourself" opens Apple's Mail app with your Pane Key attached. You send the message from your own email account; Pane itself sends nothing.

## Children

Pane collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
