# art-fill Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 11 October 2026*

art-fill does not collect any data about you.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page explains what the app does with
the things it touches, so you can check the claim rather than take it on faith.

## Your photos

When you choose a photo, iPadOS hands art-fill only the image you picked. The
app never asks for access to your photo library as a whole and cannot browse
it.

That photo is turned into a numbered canvas on your iPad, by the app itself.
It is not uploaded, not sent to a server, and not shared with anyone. There is
no server to send it to.

The photo is also kept with the painting, in the app's storage on your iPad, so
that art-fill can show it beside the finished painting in Before & After. It
stays there, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you delete the
painting or the app.

## Faces in your photos

If a photo has people in it, art-fill looks for their faces, and on each face,
for the brows and the mouth, so that it can draw them with more care. This is
done on your iPad by Apple's built-in Vision framework. It finds *where* a face
is, never *who* it is: nothing is recognised, identified, or compared with
anything else.

Where those faces and features sit in the picture is saved with that painting,
so it reopens exactly as it was left. It stays in the app's storage with the
painting, is never sent anywhere, is never used for anything else, and is
deleted when you delete the painting or the app.

## Saving to Photos

"Save to Photos" asks for add-only permission, permission to add a picture or
a video, not to read what is already there. It is requested the first time you
use it, and only then. Decline it and the rest of the app works unchanged.

## Sharing and printing

Share opens the iPad's own share sheet, and Print its own print panel. What
leaves art-fill goes where you choose to send it, by the system, when you ask.
art-fill itself sends nothing anywhere.

## What is stored, and where

Your paintings are stored on your iPad, inside the app's own storage, so you
can come back to them. Each is kept with the photo it was made from and with
the small pictures art-fill takes as you paint, which a timelapse video is made
from. They are included in your device backup if you back up your iPad, and
they are deleted when you delete the painting or the app. Nothing is stored
anywhere else.

## Purchases

Buying or restoring the full version goes through Apple's App Store. Apple
handles the payment and your Apple Account; art-fill never sees either. What
the app receives is Apple's signed confirmation that the full version is yours,
which it checks on your iPad and does not send anywhere.

## Network

art-fill itself makes no network requests. The only connection involved is the
App Store's own, when you buy or restore the full version. There are no
analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any
kind. Painting works with the iPad in aeroplane mode.

## Accounts

There are none. art-fill has no sign-in, no profile and no way to identify you.

## Children

art-fill is rated 4+ and is safe for children to use. Because it collects no
data at all, it collects no data from children either. The full version is
bought through the App Store, so Ask to Buy and Screen Time limits apply to it
as they do to anything else.

## Changes

If a future version of art-fill ever collects anything, for example if
sharing paintings online, or generating pictures in the cloud, are ever added,
this page will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store
privacy label will change with it. A version that collects nothing will never
be replaced silently by one that does.
