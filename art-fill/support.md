# art-fill Support

art-fill is a calm way to paint on iPad, at any age. A photo of someone or
somewhere you love becomes a numbered canvas you fill in yourself, one colour
at a time.

Something wrong, or something you want? Email
[ccloudcreativez3@gmail.com](mailto:ccloudcreativez3@gmail.com). It is one person,
so a reply may take a day or two, but every message is read.

When reporting a problem, these three things make it fixable: your iPad model,
your iPadOS version (Settings → General → About), and what you did just before
it went wrong.

---

## Getting started

**Starting free.** Girl with a Pearl Earring is on the first screen and free to
paint, start to finish, along with any seasonal painting that is in season. Tap
one to begin.

**Making a canvas from your own photo** (full version). Tap **Choose Photo** and
pick any picture. Converting it
takes a few seconds. Pick **Relaxing**, **Balanced** or **Detailed** first:
that sets how many areas the picture is broken into. Relaxing gives fewer,
larger areas; Detailed gives more colours and finer work.

**Painting.** Pick a colour from the numbered swatches along the bottom, then a
tool from the strip on the left:

- **Fill**, the top button. Tap an area to fill the whole of it.
- **Brush**, drag on the canvas to paint by hand.
- **Eraser**, drag to lift paint off again.

The brush and the eraser share a width slider, which appears under them while
either is selected.

**Getting around.** Two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom. Two-finger tap to
undo, or use the undo and redo buttons.

## Apple Pencil

Press harder for a wider stroke; lift off gradually for a taper. You can rest
your hand on the screen while you draw, a resting palm is ignored while the
Pencil is drawing.

## Free and full version

**What is free?**
Girl with a Pearl Earring, all year. Each seasonal painting is free in its
season: Autumn Birches (September to November), Halloween Greetings (October), Goddess Lakshmi (October and November) and Winter (December to February). Paint them start to finish and save them to
Photos; nothing in them is held back. A painting you have already started stays
open, even after its season ends.

**What does the full version add?**
Your own photos, as many as you like, and every other classic painting, the
ones with a padlock. It is one purchase: no subscription, no ads, nothing
else to buy.

**I bought the full version, but I still see padlocks.**
On a new iPad, or after reinstalling, tap any padlocked painting, then
**Restore Purchase** at the top of the sheet. Use the same Apple Account the
full version was bought with.

**My child asked to buy it, and nothing has opened yet.**
With Ask to Buy, the purchase waits for a parent to approve it. Once it is
approved, everything opens on its own, there is nothing to tap again.

## Common questions

**Which detail setting should I pick?**
**Balanced** suits most photos. **Detailed** is best for photos of people,
because faces keep their eyes, brows and smiles there. **Relaxing** gives a
quicker canvas of larger, simpler areas. When you choose a photo, the app
suggests a setting if it thinks another would suit it better.

**My photo came out soft.**
A small photo, often a copy saved from a message or a web page, is enlarged
before it is converted. That keeps shapes and faces intact, but it cannot add
detail the photo never had. The full-size original from your camera works best.

**Why won't a colour go where I want it?**
**Stay in the lines** is probably on: each colour then only paints the areas
its number calls for. Turn it off in **⋯ → Stay in the lines** to paint any
colour anywhere.

**How do I see what it is supposed to look like?**
The eye button, at the bottom of the strip on the left. It shows the finished design, and switches back
leaving your painting exactly as it was, it never fills anything in for you.

**The numbers are in the way.**
**⋯ → Show numbers** turns them off. They also fade out on their own as paint
covers them.

**Where did my painting go?**
Paintings save themselves when you leave the canvas, and appear under **Your
paintings** on the first screen. There is no save button and nothing to
remember.

**How do I keep a finished painting?**
**⋯ → Save to Photos** writes it to your photo library at full resolution,
without the numbers or outlines.

**How do I rename or delete a painting?**
In the painting, **⋯ → Rename**. On the first screen, touch and hold a painting
for **Rename** and **Delete**.

**Can I start over?**
**⋯ → Reset painting** clears the canvas. It cannot be undone, so it asks
first.

**Does it work offline?**
Painting always does. Only buying or restoring the full version needs a
connection, because that goes through the App Store.

## Requirements

iPad running iPadOS 17 or later. Apple Pencil is supported but not required;
a finger works everywhere a Pencil does.
