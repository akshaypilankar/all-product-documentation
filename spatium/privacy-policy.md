# Spatium Privacy Policy

*Effective 13 October 2026*

Spatium is made by Akshay Pilankar. In short: **Spatium doesn't collect any data.** Your photos and files never leave your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

## What Spatium collects

Nothing. Spatium has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app contains no code that sends data over the network.

## How Spatium uses your photo library

With your permission, Spatium reads your photo library **on your device** to find similar photos, blurry photos, screenshots, screen recordings and large videos. To do this it uses Apple's on-device Vision framework and, on supported devices, Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence model.

- Text recognized in screenshots is used only to choose a group, such as "Receipts", and is then discarded. It is never stored.
- Analysis results (image similarity data, quality scores and screenshot group names) are cached in the app's private storage on your device so your library doesn't need re-analyzing on every launch. This cache is excluded from device backups. You can clear it in Settings › Re-analyze Library, and deleting the app removes it.
- Spatium remembers which items you chose to keep so it won't suggest them again. That list stays on your device.
- If you add the Spatium widget, the app shares a few numbers with it through a container on your device: the space ready to clean, free storage, your review streak, how many On This Day photos there are and how many items are new to review. No photos or text are shared with the widget, including the Lock Screen widgets. On iPhone and iPad, iOS may now and then wake Spatium briefly in the background to update these numbers from your library's details, on your device.
- If you ask Siri or the Shortcuts app how much space you can free, the answer comes from those same numbers on your device. Shortcuts can open Spatium, but they can't delete anything.
- If you make a PDF from receipts or documents, the text Spatium recognizes on them is written into that PDF so you can search it, and the PDF is saved where you choose, such as the Files app or iCloud Drive. Spatium keeps no copy of the text or the PDF.
- If you turn on the weekly reminder, it is a notification scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

## Files in folders you add

If you add folders in Files, Spatium looks through those folders on your device to find copies and big old files. It reads file names, sizes and dates, and compares the contents of files of the same size to spot copies, all on your device. It keeps no list of your files: the results are gone when you quit the app.

- Spatium can only see the folders you pick in the system's folder picker. It remembers them with a bookmark the system provides, so it doesn't have to ask again. Remove a folder in Settings › Folders for Files and Spatium forgets it.
- Files stored only in iCloud Drive or another cloud service are skipped, never downloaded.
- Spatium asks before removing files, then moves them to the Trash, or to Recently Deleted in the Files app, where you can recover them. Where a location has no Trash, Spatium asks again before deleting them for good. Removing a file from iCloud Drive removes it from all your devices.

## Deleting and compressing

Spatium never deletes anything without your confirmation: iOS, iPadOS or macOS asks you to approve every deletion from your photo library. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where you can recover them for 30 days. When you compress a video, the compressed copy is saved to your library and the original goes to Recently Deleted.

If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs changes to your library, including deletions, across your devices as usual. That is handled by Apple's system, not by Spatium.

## Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Spatium receives only a confirmation that the purchase was made, and never sees your payment details.

## Children

Spatium does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
