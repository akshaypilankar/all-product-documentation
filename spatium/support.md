# Spatium Support

Need help? Email [ccloudcreativez3@gmail.com](mailto:ccloudcreativez3@gmail.com). Replies usually come within two working days.

Spatium works on iPhone, iPad and Mac (iOS, iPadOS and macOS 26 or later).

## Spatium can't see my photos

1. **iPhone and iPad:** open **Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Spatium** and choose **Full Access**.
2. **Mac:** open **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos** and turn on Spatium.
3. With **Limited Access**, Spatium only sees the photos you selected. Choose Full Access to scan your whole library.

## I deleted something by mistake

Nothing is gone for good right away. Deleted photos and videos stay in **Photos › Recently Deleted** for 30 days. Open it, select the items and tap **Recover**. Files go to the Trash, or to Recently Deleted in the Files app.

## Why does my device ask before every deletion?

That's iOS, iPadOS or macOS protecting your library: no app can delete photos without your confirmation. Spatium groups everything you chose into one prompt.

## Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Spatium analyzes everything on your device and has no servers, no accounts and no internet connections of its own. It even works in Airplane Mode.

## How does the free plan work?

You get 25 cleanups a day: deleting one item or compressing one video counts as one cleanup. Keeping items is always free. The count starts again each day.

## Unlocking and restoring Spatium Lifetime

Spatium Lifetime is a one-time purchase that removes the daily limit. There is no subscription.

- **Buy:** **Settings › Unlock Spatium Lifetime**.
- **Restore:** **Settings › Restore Purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
- One purchase unlocks Spatium on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. If Family Sharing is on, family members get it too.

## Spatium suggests a photo I want to keep

Swipe right to keep it, and Spatium won't suggest it again. To keep a whole album out of every suggestion, add it in **Settings › Protected albums**.

## Some files weren't checked

Spatium only looks in the folders you add under **Files › Add a Folder**. Files stored only in iCloud Drive or another cloud service are skipped, never downloaded. Remove a folder in **Settings › Folders for Files**.

## The widget shows old numbers

The widget updates when you open Spatium, and now and then in the background. Open Spatium once to refresh it.

## Starting over

**Settings › Re-analyze Library** clears Spatium's on-device cache and scans your library again.

## Turning off reminders

The weekly reminder is off unless you turn it on. Switch it off in **Settings › Weekly reminder**, or in your device's notification settings.

## Deleting your data

Everything Spatium knows is stored on your device. Deleting Spatium deletes it all. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

## Privacy

Spatium collects no data. Read the full [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
