## The microphone misses notes

1. Run **Test Your Piano** from Home. It checks how quiet the room is, then listens for one key.
2. Put your iPhone or iPad on the music stand, close to the strings or speakers, and play each key firmly.
3. Let go of keys you've finished with, and go easy on the sustain pedal. A note still ringing can hide the next one.
4. The microphone follows one or two notes at a time best. For pieces with bigger chords, pick the **Easy** or **Medium** version of the song, or connect a MIDI keyboard.
5. **Wait mode** is the most forgiving: the music waits until it hears the right note.

## Connecting a MIDI keyboard (Pro)

Tap the keyboard button at the top of Home to open **Piano Connection**.

- **USB-C iPhone or iPad:** plug the keyboard straight in. If it doesn't light up, connect it through a powered USB hub.
- **iPhone with a Lightning port:** use Apple's Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter with a charger plugged into it. A plain Lightning-to-USB cable can't connect a keyboard.
- **Bluetooth:** tap **Pair a Bluetooth MIDI keyboard**, and turn on Bluetooth MIDI on the keyboard first.

Some keyboards powered over USB need more power than an iPhone gives out. A powered hub fixes that.

## Easy, Medium and Full

Every song comes in up to three versions:

- **Easy:** just the tune, in the right hand.
- **Medium:** one note at a time in each hand, the tune and the bass.
- **Full:** every note.

Switch between them with **Version** in the song's practice settings.

## Learn step by step (Pro)

Each song gets a plan: a few bars at a time, one hand then both, then slowly up to full speed. On devices with Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple Intelligence writes the tips for each step, on your device. On other devices, the plan works the same with built-in tips.

## Importing your own songs

Tap **Import a MIDI file** under **My songs**, or open a MIDI file from Files, AirDrop or Mail and choose Chopin Piano. Imported songs stay on your device.

## Practice reminders

Tap the bell on the **Today's practice** card to turn the daily reminder on or off, or to change its time. You can also turn off all notifications in **Settings › Notifications › Chopin Piano**.

## Restoring or cancelling Pro

- **Restore:** open the Pro screen and tap **Restore purchases**, signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with.
- **Cancel a subscription:** tap **Manage subscription** on the Pro screen, or go to **Settings › your name › Subscriptions**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
- **Free trial:** Chopin Piano reminds you two days before a trial turns into a paid subscription.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

Wait mode, the microphone and the first 15 songs are always free.

## Languages

Chopin Piano is in English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Hindi, and follows your device's language. To pick a language just for Chopin Piano, go to **Settings › Apps › Chopin Piano › Language**.

## Deleting your data

Your practice history is stored on your device. Deleting Chopin Piano deletes it.

## Privacy

Chopin Piano has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Read the full [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) and the [terms of use](terms-of-use.md).
