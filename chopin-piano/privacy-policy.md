# Chopin Piano Privacy Policy

*Effective 11 October 2026*

Chopin Piano is made by Akshay Pilankar. In short: **Chopin Piano has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.** It doesn't collect personal data, and nothing you play is sent to me.

## Microphone

The app listens to your piano to check the notes you play. Sound is analysed on your device as it arrives and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

## MIDI keyboards and Bluetooth

Key presses from a connected keyboard are used only on your device. Bluetooth is used only to connect MIDI keyboards.

## Practice history

Your scores, practice time and streaks are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Imported songs

MIDI files you import are turned into songs on your device and stay there. They're never uploaded.

## Song downloads

New songs are downloaded from the Chopin Piano website, which is hosted by GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub receives your device's IP address when the app downloads songs; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The app sends nothing else, and I don't receive these logs.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are handled by Apple. I never see your payment details; the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Chopin Piano sends two kinds, both scheduled on your device:

- a practice reminder at the time you choose, if you turn it on, and only on days you haven't practised yet
- a reminder before a free trial turns into a paid subscription

## Children

Chopin Piano collects no personal information from anyone, including children, so it's suitable for all ages.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and in the app, with a new effective date.
