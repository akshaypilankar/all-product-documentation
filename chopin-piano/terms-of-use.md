## Feedback and tips

Chopin Piano is a learning aid. The microphone can miss or mishear notes, so scores and feedback are a guide, not a judgement of your playing. On devices with Apple Intelligence, some tips are written by Apple Intelligence on your device and can occasionally be wrong. Take breaks, and stop if playing hurts.
